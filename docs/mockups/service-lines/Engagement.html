Why an engagement, not a folder

Compass's existing ownership rule separates users — it was never a tenancy boundary, and its own source says so.

Client tax data needs the stronger thing. The engagement is where residency, retention, the access list and the independence verdict all attach, so there is one object to point at when somebody asks who could see what.
