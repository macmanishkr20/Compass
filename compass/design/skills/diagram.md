Draw a reference-architecture diagram — a technical drawing, not an illustration. Work in this order, and say nothing about the order in the output.
1. Decide the zones: the trust or ownership boundaries the nodes sit in (a tenant, a network, an edge, an observability estate). Two to four of them, left to right in the order the flow visits them.
2. Put the nodes in their zones, top to bottom in flow order.
3. Number the flows 1..N in the order they happen.
4. Write the numbered steps out underneath.

How it is built — follow this exactly, it is what keeps a diagram from collapsing into a tangle:
- Lay the drawing out with CSS grid, not coordinates. The stage is a grid of zone columns with a gap of at least 56px; each zone is a grid or flex column of node cards with a gap of at least 28px and 24px of padding. Nothing is absolutely positioned, so nothing can overlap: the browser does the placing.
- Give every node an id. Declare the flows once as data — an array of {from, to, n, label} in a <script>. The flows and the numbered steps in the legend are the same list: every step is a drawn connector and every connector is a step, one to one, in the same order. A card with no flow through it does not belong in the drawing.
- Draw the connectors at runtime. Wrap the stage in a positioned figure and put the <svg> overlay in as the stage's SIBLING, not inside it — absolutely positioned over it, pointer-events: none, behind the cards. The script measures each node with getBoundingClientRect against the figure and draws, for every flow, an orthogonal path: out of the source's edge, along the gutter between the columns, then into the target's edge. Only M, H, V and L — no curves, no diagonals. End each in a small filled arrowhead from a <marker>, and put a filled circle with the step number at the path's midpoint.
- Redraw on load, on resize (ResizeObserver on the stage) and when the stage's contents change (MutationObserver on the STAGE only). Clear the overlay first so redrawing is idempotent, and debounce through requestAnimationFrame. The overlay must never be inside what the observer watches: an observer that sees its own drawing redraws for ever and the page never finishes loading. The page must reach load and settle — no redraw may trigger another.
- Every flow gets its own channel — offset each run 14px from the last so two lines never share a track and two badges never stack. A line leaves its source card's edge at the card's middle and arrives at the target card's edge the same way, so it is obvious which two cards a line joins. Between those two points it travels only in the gaps — down or up to the gutter between two rows, along it, then in to the target. A line never crosses a card and a badge never sits on card text; the overlay stays behind the cards so even a mistake reads as a line passing under. Keep every line out of the top 26px of a zone, where its label lives.

How it reads:
- A header: a small letterspaced uppercase kicker, the title, and a right-aligned block of LABEL / value metadata rows, over a hairline rule.
- Zones are rounded rectangles behind their nodes: a very light tinted fill, a 1px border — dashed where the boundary is a trust boundary — and an uppercase letterspaced label at the top left, three words at most, never wrapping.
- Nodes are white cards: 1px border, 6px radius, the name in bold, one or two muted detail lines under it, and a small square icon slot in the top-right corner. A card's height comes from its text — never a fixed height, and never text spilling past the border. Columns are wide enough to read: at least 240px of card, so no line of a card's text breaks after one or two words.
- Under the drawing, a legend headed with the flow's name: the numbered steps in two or three columns, each a single short sentence. The one constraint that matters most closes the legend as its own line under the columns — never as a numbered step. That line is about the system — what must hold for it to be safe or correct — never about the drawing or these instructions.
- The legend is below the drawing and clear of it: the stage ends, then the legend begins. Neither may overlap the other.
- Monospace for every label, name and detail; the title may be a sans. Unless a design system says otherwise the palette is fixed: ink #12283F, accent #2F6F9F for every connector, arrowhead and numbered badge, zone tint #EEF4FA, card borders #CFE0EE, white cards. No greens, no second accent, no shadows on the nodes, no gradients, and no background grid or graph paper.
