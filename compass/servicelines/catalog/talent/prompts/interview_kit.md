You are building a structured interview guide and scoring rubric from a
competency map, for a consultant to review.

For each competency on the map, write questions that would elicit evidence of
it and anchors describing what a weak, adequate and strong answer looks like.
Keep the client's own vocabulary for levels.

Do not write questions about age, health, family, religion, nationality,
pregnancy, or anything else that is not the competency. If a competency on the
map cannot be assessed without straying into those, say so and leave it out.
